Questions

Where is Newport Smog & Auto Registration?

We are at 375 Bristol St, Unit 80, Costa Mesa, CA 92626. Look for the “Newport Smog Test Only” sign.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed on Sunday.

Are you a STAR certified smog station?

Yes. We are a STAR certified, test-only smog check station, BAR # ARD 269691.

What does test-only mean?

We only test vehicles. We don't do repairs, so there is no repair upsell.

What vehicles do you smog check?

We smog check vehicles from 1976 to the present that run on gasoline, diesel, CNG or hybrid power, including cars, vans, SUVs, trucks and RVs. We also smog high-performance and exotic cars.

Do you offer DMV and auto registration services?

Yes. We handle registration renewals, title transfers, non-resident transfers, duplicate titles, duplicate license plates, duplicate registrations and stickers, and VIN verification.

Do I need an appointment?

You don't have to book ahead. You can check in online before you arrive at newportsmog.com/checkin, book an appointment at checkin.newportsmog.com, or call (714) 436-1700.

How do I get a quote?

Call (714) 436-1700 or stop by the shop for a quote.
